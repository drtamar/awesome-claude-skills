# Art Movements Reference

Each entry: period · visual signature · color tendencies · composition · how to replicate digitally.

---

## Renaissance (1400–1600)
**Visual signature:** Sfumato (Leonardo), balanced pyramidal composition, naturalistic human figures, chiaroscuro (strong light/shadow), linear perspective, oil glazing technique creates luminous depth.
**Colors:** Warm earth tones — Venetian red `#8B1A1A`, ochre `#C8922A`, ultramarine `#003366`, lead white `#F5F0E8`, ivory black `#1C1C18`. Rich saturated pigments.
**Composition:** Triangle/pyramid groupings, central vanishing point, figures arranged in shallow stage-like space, landscape backgrounds recede in atmospheric perspective.
**Replicate as:** Deep shadows with soft edge transitions, warm amber light source from upper-left, muted background with cooler aerial perspective, figures modeled with smooth tonal gradients.

---

## Baroque (1600–1750)
**Visual signature:** Extreme chiaroscuro (tenebrism in Caravaggio), dramatic diagonal compositions, emotional intensity, movement and tension, ornate detail, theatrical lighting from single unseen source.
**Colors:** Very dark backgrounds `#0D0A06`, golden light `#E8C050`, warm flesh `#C4956A`, deep crimson `#8B0000`, dramatic value contrast.
**Composition:** Strong diagonals cutting across frame, figures emerging from deep shadow into focused light (spotlight effect), complex overlapping forms, dynamic S-curves.
**Replicate as:** Near-black background, single harsh directional light, very high contrast, warm key light against cool deep shadow, no ambient fill light.

---

## Rococo (1720–1780)
**Visual signature:** Pastel palette, playful and frivolous scenes, asymmetric ornate decoration, soft feathery brushwork (Fragonard), lightness vs. Baroque drama, curved S and C shapes everywhere.
**Colors:** Powder blue `#B0C4DE`, blush rose `#FFB6C1`, pale gold `#F5DEB3`, sage green `#8FBC8F`, ivory `#FFFFF0`. Everything desaturated and light.
**Composition:** Off-center, asymmetric, swirling rhythms, lush foliage framing, figures in leisure poses, sky takes up much of the canvas.
**Replicate as:** Soft pastel palette, low contrast, feathered edge transitions, decorative floral/scroll borders, airy spacing.

---

## Neoclassicism (1760–1850)
**Visual signature:** Greek/Roman architectural references, moral seriousness (David), clean outline over color, frieze-like horizontal compositions, smooth "licked" surface finish, idealized forms.
**Colors:** Stone white `#F2EFE4`, Pompeii red `#C14936`, Prussian blue `#003153`, marble grey `#C0B9A8`, gold `#CFB53B`. Cooler and more restrained than Baroque.
**Composition:** Horizontal frieze arrangement, figures parallel to picture plane, architectural setting establishes rational space, strong geometric structure.
**Replicate as:** Cool clean palette, hard precise edges, symmetrical layout, Roman/Greek architectural motifs, minimal texture.

---

## Romanticism (1800–1850)
**Visual signature:** Sublime nature dwarfing humans (Friedrich, Turner), emotional drama, stormy skies, exotic subjects, loose expressive brushwork, awe-inspiring scale.
**Colors:** Turner's atmospheric light — sulfurous yellow `#FFD700`, storm grey `#708090`, deep crimson `#8B0000`, mist white `#F8F8FF`, deep shadow blue-black `#1C2333`.
**Composition:** Humans tiny vs. vast landscape, strong atmospheric perspective (foreground dark, distance dissolves into light/mist), diagonal storm lines, radiant horizon.
**Replicate as:** Dramatic sky dominates, atmospheric haze, high contrast between dark foreground and luminous distance, loose visible brushwork texture.

---

## Realism (1840–1880)
**Visual signature:** Ordinary working-class subjects (Courbet, Millet), anti-idealization, earthy palette, honest unglamorous light, thick impasto texture.
**Colors:** Earth tones dominate — raw umber `#826644`, burnt sienna `#8A3324`, field green `#556B2F`, grey sky `#B0B8C0`, unidealized flesh.
**Composition:** Eye-level viewpoint (not heroic low angle), workers filling the frame, natural lighting without drama, no theatrical gestures.
**Replicate as:** Muted earth palette, natural diffuse lighting, rough texture, unglamorous subject matter, straightforward framing.

---

## Impressionism (1860–1886)
**Visual signature:** Broken brushwork capturing light at a moment (Monet, Renoir), color in shadow (violet/blue instead of grey/black), painting outdoors (en plein air), dissolution of hard edges, optical color mixing.
**Colors:** Shadow is never black — use violet `#8B00FF` + blue `#0000CD`. Light: warm yellows `#FFE066`, orange `#FFA500`. Greens: varied `#228B22` to `#90EE90`. Pure unmixed pigments placed side by side.
**Composition:** Informal cropping (influenced by photography), figures cut by frame edge, flat decorative areas of color, atmospheric over linear perspective.
**Replicate as:** Dabs of unmixed color side by side, visible brushstrokes, soft unfocused quality, colored shadows (violet/blue-purple), no outlines, shimmering edges.

---

## Post-Impressionism (1886–1905)
Four distinct sub-styles:

**Cézanne** — Geometric underlying structure, multiple viewpoints simultaneously, warm/cool color modeling instead of light/shadow, constructive parallel brushstrokes.
Colors: Grey-green `#8FAF8F`, warm ochre `#C8922A`, Provençal terra cotta `#C1654C`.

**Van Gogh** — Swirling dynamic brushstrokes expressing emotion, complementary color pairs (yellow/violet, orange/blue), thick impasto, dark outlines around forms.
Colors: Chrome yellow `#FFD700`, Prussian blue `#003153`, cypress green `#2D5A1B`, orange `#FF8C00`. Intense saturated.

**Gauguin** — Flat areas of pure color, dark outlines (cloisonnism), exotic tropical palette, symbolic color (non-naturalistic), primitive simplified forms.
Colors: Tropical red `#DC143C`, mango yellow `#FFB300`, deep teal `#00695C`, warm brown skin `#8D5524`.

**Seurat** — Pointillism: tiny dots of pure color optically mix at distance, very systematic grid-like application, precise light science.
Colors: Pure spectrum colors applied as dots — the canvas surface itself is visible.

---

## Fauvism (1905–1910)
**Visual signature:** Color as pure emotional expression (Matisse, Derain), non-naturalistic — red trees, green faces — color divorced from representation, flat simplified forms, bold black outlines.
**Colors:** Arbitrary and pure — cadmium red `#FF3030`, viridian `#40826D`, cobalt blue `#0047AB`, chrome yellow `#FFD700`. Maximum saturation, no mixing to neutrals.
**Composition:** Flat pattern-like, decorative, simplified shapes, strong outline, compressed depth.
**Replicate as:** Pure unmixed saturated colors, bold black outlines, flat areas, deliberately non-realistic color choices, decorative pattern quality.

---

## Expressionism (1905–1930)
**Visual signature:** Inner emotional state over outer reality (Munch, Kirchner, Schiele), distorted forms, angular lines, harsh color, psychological tension, woodblock-influenced flatness.
**Colors:** Jaundiced yellows `#CCCC00`, harsh greens `#00CC00`, blood reds `#CC0000`, anxious purples `#6600CC`, sickly flesh `#DEB887`.
**Composition:** Tilted vertiginous space, exaggerated gestures, compressed claustrophobic framing, angular fragmented background, figure dominates frame.
**Replicate as:** Distorted proportions, harsh non-naturalistic color, angular forms, psychological tension in pose, dark oppressive atmosphere.

---

## Cubism (1907–1920)
**Visual signature:** Multiple simultaneous viewpoints (Picasso, Braque), geometric fragmentation of form, muted palette (Analytic) or collage-like color (Synthetic), overlap of time and space, two-dimensionality emphasized.
**Colors:** Analytic: grey `#808080`, brown `#8B6914`, ochre `#C8922A`, near-monochromatic. Synthetic: brighter — newspaper grey + colorful collage elements.
**Composition:** Fractured planes stacked and overlapping, no single vanishing point, shallow compressed depth, forms analyzed into geometric facets.
**Replicate as:** Fragment forms into geometric facets, multiple profiles of same object shown simultaneously, muted nearly monochromatic palette, overlapping transparent planes.

---

## Futurism (1909–1920s)
**Visual signature:** Speed, movement, technology worship (Boccioni, Balla), dynamism through multiplied sequential forms, force lines radiating from center, urban industrial subjects.
**Colors:** Dynamic and energetic — electric blue `#007FFF`, speed yellow `#FFD700`, machinery grey `#808080`, motion blur white.
**Composition:** Radiating force lines from center, sequential repeated forms showing motion path, diagonal energy, compressed dynamic tension.
**Replicate as:** Motion blur effect, repeated sequential shapes, radiating diagonal lines, energy and speed in every stroke direction.

---

## Dada (1916–1924)
**Visual signature:** Anti-art, photomontage, found objects, absurdist juxtaposition, intentional irrationality, typographic play, chance operations.
**Colors:** Newspaper grey, primary colors used flatly, photographic black and white + hand color, jarring combinations.
**Composition:** Collage logic — cut and paste, text overlapping image, random scale relationships, deliberate visual chaos with underlying wit.
**Replicate as:** Photomontage aesthetic, mixed typography at different angles/scales, stark black and white with selective color, irrational spatial relationships.

---

## Surrealism (1924–1950s)
**Visual signature:** Dream logic (Dalí, Magritte, Ernst), hyperrealistic rendering of impossible scenes, unexpected juxtapositions, biomorphic forms (Miró), automatic drawing, double images.
**Colors:** Dalí: realistic naturalistic color makes the impossible more uncanny. Magritte: everyday color in wrong context. Miró: primary colors on cream/black, biomorphic shapes.
**Composition:** Deep illusionistic space (Dalí) or flat symbolic space (Miró), isolated objects with impossible shadows, scale disruption, objects melting or transforming.
**Replicate as:** Photorealistic rendering of impossible objects, dreamlike scale inconsistencies, deep blue sky with strange elements, meticulous detail applied to absurdity.

---

## Abstract Expressionism (1940s–1950s)
Two major modes:

**Action Painting (Pollock, de Kooning):** Paint application IS the content — drips, splashes, gestural marks, all-over composition (no focal point), large scale, physicality of paint.
Colors: Full spectrum in Pollock, raw gestural marks. No single palette.

**Color Field (Rothko, Newman):** Large luminous fields of color with soft edges, meditative and emotional, simple geometry, transcendence through pure color.
Colors: Rothko — dark reds `#8B0000`, maroon, deep orange `#FF4500`, dark sienna, glowing edges. Saturated but deep.
**Replicate as:** Color field: two or three large soft-edged rectangles of closely valued color, slightly lighter glow at edges, very large format.

---

## Pop Art (1950s–1970s)
**Visual signature:** Mass media imagery elevated to fine art (Warhol, Lichtenstein, Hockney), Ben-Day dots (Lichtenstein), silkscreen flat color (Warhol), celebrity/consumer products as subject.
**Colors:** Warhol: arbitrary flat screenprint color — hot pink `#FF69B4`, cyan `#00FFFF`, yellow `#FFFF00`, black outline. Lichtenstein: primary red/blue/yellow + Ben-Day dots.
**Composition:** Bold simple outlines, flat color fills, centered iconic imagery, repetition/grid (Warhol), comic book speech bubbles (Lichtenstein).
**Replicate as:** Black outlines, flat solid color fills, halftone dot pattern (Ben-Day), bold simple imagery, repetition grid, bright primary palette.

---

## Minimalism (1960s–1970s)
**Visual signature:** Reduction to essential geometric form (Judd, Flavin, LeWitt), industrial materials, serial repetition, no illusion or metaphor, object is itself.
**Colors:** Industrial — steel grey, white, black, single pure color. No mixing, no gradients. Fluorescent (Flavin).
**Composition:** Grid, simple repeated geometric unit, no hierarchy, serial logic, edge-to-edge composition.
**Replicate as:** Pure geometry, monochromatic or limited palette, strict grid/repetition, no texture, no expressive marks.

---

## Street Art / Graffiti (1970s–present)
**Visual signature:** Wildstyle lettering, bubble letters, throw-ups, blockbusters, stencil art (Banksy), wheatpaste, character pieces, site-specific context.
**Colors:** Spray paint palette — bold outlines in black, fill in bright saturated colors, chrome silver highlights, glow/halo effects.
**Composition:** Large scale to fill architectural surface, letter-based or character-based, outline + fill + highlight structure, background often atmospheric.
**Replicate as:** Bold black outlines, bright fill colors, white highlights simulating spray chrome, textured background suggesting concrete/brick.

---

## Digital / Glitch Art (1990s–present)
**Visual signature:** Intentional digital corruption — pixel sorting, datamoshing, JPEG artifacts, RGB channel shift, scan line disruption, 3D render glitches.
**Colors:** Full RGB spectrum with chromatic aberration shifts — red channel offset left, blue channel offset right by 3–8px, heavy saturation.
**Composition:** Horizontal corruption bands, pixel sorting creates vertical streaks, original image still partially recognizable, digital error as aesthetic.
**Replicate as:** CSS `mix-blend-mode`, RGB channel offsets, pixel sort algorithms, compression artifact simulation, scan lines.
