# Art Masters — Style Signatures

When a user references an artist by name, apply these specific visual rules.
Format: movement · signature techniques · palette · composition habits · replication notes.

---

## Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519)
**Movement:** High Renaissance
**Signature:** Sfumato (smoke-like tonal transitions, no hard edges), chiaroscuro, pyramid compositions, ambiguous expression (Mona Lisa smile), scientific naturalism, left-hand hatching in drawings.
**Palette:** Warm amber `#C8922A`, deep shadow brown `#3D1C02`, sfumato grey-green `#8A9A7B`, flesh `#DEB887`, lapis blue (sparingly) `#1F305E`.
**Composition:** Pyramidal grouping of figures, landscape background with atmospheric haze, soft vignette edge, three-quarter portrait pose at slight angle.
**Replication:** Soft-edge everything (blur radius 8–15px on transitions), warm amber light from upper right, backgrounds dissolve into grey-green haze, no sharp outlines anywhere.

---

## Michelangelo (1475–1564)
**Movement:** High Renaissance / Mannerism
**Signature:** Muscular idealized male figures (terribilità), non-finito (intentionally unfinished marble), complex twisting poses (contrapposto extreme), ceiling fresco scale.
**Palette:** Stone white `#F0EBD8`, warm flesh `#C4956A`, earth shadow `#6B4226`, fresco faded blue `#7A9EB5`, warm yellow ochre `#C8922A`.
**Composition:** Single heroic figure dominating frame, strong internal diagonal twist (serpentine), dramatic foreshortening, monumental scale emphasis.
**Replication:** Emphasize musculature with strong highlight/shadow, warm stone ground, single figure with powerful contrapposto, minimal background.

---

## Rembrandt van Rijn (1606–1669)
**Movement:** Dutch Golden Age / Baroque
**Signature:** Rembrandt lighting (triangle of light on shadow-side cheek), impasto highlights, dark background emerging into warmly lit subject, psychological depth in portraits, self-portraits obsession.
**Palette:** Background: near-black `#0A0806`. Key light: golden amber `#D4A853`. Shadow: warm dark brown `#2D1A0A`. Highlight: cream white `#FFF8DC`. Deep red accent `#6B0F1A`.
**Composition:** Sitter emerges from deep shadow, single light source from upper left, three-quarter face with one side lit, background undefined darkness, clothing catches light.
**Replication:** Near-black background, one warm amber spotlight, Rembrandt triangle on cheek, thick impasto highlight on forehead, everything else dissolves into warm shadow.

---

## Johannes Vermeer (1632–1675)
**Movement:** Dutch Golden Age
**Signature:** Window light from left casting cool diffuse illumination, domestic interior scenes, pointillés (tiny dots of white representing light sparkle), camera obscura optical quality, pearl earrings and blue/yellow costumes.
**Palette:** Lapis lazuli blue `#1B3A6B`, lead yellow `#E8D44D`, cool window light `#D0E4F7`, warm shadow `#8B6914`, milky white `#F5F0E8`.
**Composition:** Window always at left edge, figure occupies middle third, simple back wall, table in foreground, optical lens-like quality (slightly softened depth of field effect).
**Replication:** Cool blue-white light from left window, warm amber interior shadows on right, the quality of diffused northern light, hyper-clean and detailed surfaces.

---

## Francisco Goya (1746–1828)
**Movement:** Romanticism / Expressionism precursor
**Signature:** Two modes: elegant court portraits (early) vs. dark nightmarish "Black Paintings" (late). Saturn Devouring His Son: raw terror, dark palette, nightmare figures, loose expressionistic late style.
**Palette (Dark period):** Near-black `#0D0A06`, ochre `#8B6914`, bone white `#F5F0DC`, dried blood `#5C1A1A`, murky olive `#3D3D00`.
**Composition (Dark):** Figures pressed against picture plane, no rational space, terror in faces, no light source logic, claustrophobic.
**Replication:** Very dark desaturated palette, raw painterly texture, figures looming close to viewer, psychological dread in expression.

---

## J.M.W. Turner (1775–1851)
**Movement:** Romanticism
**Signature:** Light as subject itself, forms dissolve into radiant atmosphere, vortex compositions, steam/smoke/mist, color temperature drama (cool shadow, burning warm light), increasingly abstract late works.
**Palette:** Burning yellow `#FFD700`, sulfur `#FFEC8B`, ice blue `#B0C4DE`, storm grey `#708090`, deep shadow purple `#1C1450`, red-orange `#FF4500`.
**Composition:** Central radiant light source (often sun or explosion), forms spiral around vortex center, horizon divides canvas roughly in thirds, foreground dark framing device.
**Replication:** Radial gradient of light from center, dissolve all hard edges into atmosphere, warm burning core with cool misty periphery, no sharp outlines.

---

## Édouard Manet (1832–1883)
**Movement:** Realism / Proto-Impressionism
**Signature:** Flat broad brushwork eliminating halftones (directly from light to dark), confrontational gaze, modern Parisian subjects, black as a color not an absence, flattening depth, sketchy backgrounds.
**Palette:** Strong black `#1A1A1A`, pure white `#FAFAFA`, warm flesh `#DEB887`, Paris green `#50C878`, champagne `#F7E7CE`.
**Composition:** Figure confronts viewer directly (no looking away), compressed shallow space, bold value contrast with few intermediate tones, flat treatment of background.
**Replication:** High contrast between light and dark with minimal midtones, broad confident brushwork, figure facing viewer, modern setting.

---

## Claude Monet (1840–1926)
**Movement:** Impressionism
**Signature:** Series paintings (same subject in different light), water reflections, broken color, lily pads, Giverny garden, colour of atmosphere not local colour of objects, late works nearly abstract.
**Palette:** Monet series: cool morning — blue `#87CEEB`, violet `#9370DB`. Noon: green `#90EE90`, yellow. Sunset: orange `#FFA500`, pink `#FFB6C1`. Water: mirror of sky + green-brown depths.
**Composition:** Horizon often eliminated (water series), reflections as mirror composition, lily pads as surface pattern, soft unfocused quality throughout.
**Replication:** Broken dabs of adjacent complementary colors, colored shadows (violet), soft unfocused quality, water reflection as second inverted composition.

---

## Paul Cézanne (1839–1906)
**Movement:** Post-Impressionism
**Signature:** Multiple simultaneous viewpoints (proto-cubism), parallel constructive brushstrokes, modulation of warm/cool to create form (not light/shadow), tilted table tops, apples and Mont Sainte-Victoire.
**Palette:** Warm ochre `#C8922A`, cool grey-green `#8FAF8F`, Provençal blue sky `#4682B4`, earth red `#A0522D`, apple red-green `#8B4513`.
**Composition:** Slightly tilted table (multiple eye levels), objects crowded together without cast shadows, flattened depth, mountain as geometric mass.
**Replication:** Parallel hatching brushstrokes in organized directions, warm/cool color alternation for modeling, compressed space, geometric underlying structure.

---

## Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890)
**Movement:** Post-Impressionism
**Signature:** Swirling expressive brushstrokes (The Starry Night), thick impasto, complementary color pairs (yellow/violet, orange/blue), emotional use of color, dark outlines, intense psychological energy.
**Palette:** Chrome yellow `#FFD700`, Prussian blue `#003153`, cypress green `#2D5A1B`, orange `#FF8C00`, violet `#7B2D8B`, red `#CC2200`. Maximum saturation.
**Composition:** Dynamic swirling sky, cypress trees as vertical anchors, village below calm vs. turbulent sky, thick paint surface catches light.
**Replication:** Directional visible brushstrokes that swirl and flow, intense saturated complementary colors, thick texture, dark outlines around major forms.

---

## Paul Gauguin (1848–1903)
**Movement:** Post-Impressionism / Symbolism
**Signature:** Tahitian subjects, cloisonnism (thick dark outlines like stained glass), flat areas of pure non-naturalistic color, decorative pattern, mystical/symbolic content, warm tropical palette.
**Palette:** Tropical red `#DC143C`, mango yellow `#FFB300`, deep teal `#00695C`, warm brown skin `#8D5524`, lime green `#32CD32`, cobalt blue `#0047AB`.
**Composition:** Frieze-like horizontal arrangement, figures flattened against background, decorative overall pattern, dark outline separating color fields.
**Replication:** Thick black outlines, flat pure color fills (no modeling), tropical warm palette, decorative pattern quality, figures simplified to flat shapes.

---

## Gustav Klimt (1862–1918)
**Movement:** Art Nouveau / Vienna Secession
**Signature:** Gold leaf decoration, erotic subjects in ornate pattern, mosaic-like fragmentation of costume, portraits emerge from abstract decorative field, The Kiss, Woman in Gold.
**Palette:** Gold `#D4AF37`, black `#0D0D0D`, white `#FAFAFA`, red `#CC2200`, turquoise `#00CED1`, ivory `#FFFFF0`. Gold dominates.
**Composition:** Face and hands painted naturalistically, body dissolves into gold mosaic pattern, portrait floats in abstract decorative field, square or tall portrait format.
**Replication:** Naturalistic face and hands, geometric mosaic pattern replacing garments/background in gold and jewel tones, Art Nouveau organic decorative border.

---

## Egon Schiele (1890–1918)
**Movement:** Expressionism
**Signature:** Contorted angular figures, raw sexuality, deliberate ugliness of body, scratchy aggressive line, isolated figures against blank backgrounds, self-portraits.
**Palette:** Sickly flesh `#DEB887` with orange `#FF6347` and green tinges, raw sienna `#A0522D`, bone white `#F5F0DC`, scratchy black line, smudgy charcoal.
**Composition:** Figure isolated against stark white or cream, figure fills frame awkwardly, contorted pose emphasizing joints and angularity, no background setting.
**Replication:** Raw scratchy outlines, angular distorted proportions, sickly warm-cool flesh tones, no background, psychological discomfort in pose.

---

## Salvador Dalí (1904–1989)
**Movement:** Surrealism
**Signature:** Hyperrealistic rendering of impossible scenarios, melting clocks, Catalan landscape (Cap de Creus), double images, paranoiac-critical method, meticulous academic technique applied to dreams.
**Palette:** Catalan golden light `#D4A017`, hot shadow blue `#00008B`, burnt sienna desert `#A0522D`, sky blue `#87CEEB`, flesh `#DEB887`. Naturalistic but dreamlike.
**Composition:** Deep perspectival space, tiny figures in vast landscape, impossible objects in exact shadow, horizon very low, objects suspended or melting.
**Replication:** Photorealistic rendering of impossible things, deep receding landscape, very precise shadow and reflection of objects that shouldn't exist.

---

## Frida Kahlo (1907–1954)
**Movement:** Surrealism / Folk Art / Symbolism
**Signature:** Self-portraits, Mexican folk art tradition, personal suffering as subject (accidents, surgeries), symbolic objects, unibrow, traditional Tehuana dress, animals.
**Palette:** Tropical Mexican palette — deep red `#CC2200`, turquoise `#00CED1`, gold `#D4AF37`, forest green `#228B22`, warm brown skin, cobalt blue background `#0047AB`.
**Composition:** Close frontal portrait, symmetric stiff pose (influenced by ex-votos), symbolic objects surrounding figure, detailed botanical elements, often self-contained like a shrine painting.
**Replication:** Frontal symmetrical portrait, folk art flatness, vivid saturated color, symbolic objects placed around figure, decorative floral elements.

---

## Mark Rothko (1903–1970)
**Movement:** Abstract Expressionism / Color Field
**Signature:** Two or three large soft-edged rectangles of closely valued color, edges glow and breathe, luminosity from color beneath (underpainting shows through), emotional/spiritual content through pure color.
**Palette:** Rothko's signature: dark reds `#8B0000` + maroon `#800000` + deep orange `#CC4400`. Also: black + dark grey series. Color relationships between rectangles create the meaning.
**Composition:** Two or three horizontal rectangles stacked, very narrow margins, rectangles nearly fill canvas, edges intentionally soft and uneven, no hard geometry.
**Replication:** Two or three large color fields with soft blurred edges between them, subtle color variation within each field, slight glow at edges, large scale critical.

---

## Andy Warhol (1928–1987)
**Movement:** Pop Art
**Signature:** Silkscreen repetition, celebrity faces as commodity (Marilyn, Mao), arbitrary color substitution, misregistration of color layers as aesthetic, Campbell's soup, Factory production.
**Palette:** Arbitrary and flat — hot pink `#FF69B4`, cyan `#00FFFF`, lime `#00FF00`, orange `#FF6600`, yellow `#FFFF00`. Different palette each repeat. Black for outlines.
**Composition:** Centered face or object, square format, grid repetition (multiple same image with color variation), screenprint flat fills with slight misregistration.
**Replication:** Flat color fills, black outline layer, deliberately offset color from outline slightly, repeat in grid with different color per cell.

---

## Roy Lichtenstein (1923–1997)
**Movement:** Pop Art
**Signature:** Ben-Day dots (newspaper halftone pattern), bold black outlines, primary colors only, comic book source imagery, speech bubbles, brushstroke paintings, Benday dot pattern screen.
**Palette:** Ben-Day red `#FF0000`, blue `#0000FF`, yellow `#FFFF00`, black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`. Nothing else — no mixtures.
**Composition:** Comic panel cropping, close-up on face or action, bold diagonal speed lines, speech bubble text, dot pattern fills shadow areas.
**Replication:** Black outlines (3–5px), solid primary fills, Ben-Day dot pattern (circle grid) for shadows/skin, no gradient, comic book speech bubbles.

---

## Jean-Michel Basquiat (1960–1988)
**Movement:** Neo-Expressionism / Street Art
**Signature:** Crowns (SAMO tag), crossed-out words and rewriting, anatomical figures with exposed skull/organs, text as image, raw gestural marks, Black cultural identity, Haitian references.
**Palette:** Raw canvas/cardboard showing through, black scratchy marks `#1A1A1A`, red `#CC0000`, yellow `#FFD700`, blue `#0000CC`, brown `#8B4513`. Raw and unfinished.
**Composition:** All-over with no clear hierarchy, text overlaid on image, diagrams and figures coexist, multiple layers visible, raw materiality.
**Replication:** Scratchy raw lines, layered text over image, raw background showing, crowns as recurring motif, unfinished intentional quality.

---

## Banksy (1974–)
**Movement:** Street Art / Political Art
**Signature:** Stencil technique (hard edge, spray paint), subversive political message, incongruous juxtaposition (child + bomb, soldier + flower), site-specific intervention, anonymity.
**Palette:** Black stencil on wall grey/white `#808080`, single flat color accent (red `#CC0000` for balloon/heart, green for soldier, etc.), spray paint texture.
**Composition:** Simple clear stencil silhouette, one image tells the political story, minimal elements maximum impact, context of wall/location is part of the meaning.
**Replication:** High contrast black silhouette, one accent color, stencil edge quality, simple readable image from distance, political/ironic caption.
